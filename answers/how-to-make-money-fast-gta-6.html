  2. Hours 5–15 (Chapter 3): Complete the Port Vice heist. Buy auto shop ($120,000). Run vehicle theft-to-order missions between story missions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do you start with in GTA 6?

Jason and Lucia start with separate small cash reserves tied to their backgrounds — enough for basic supplies but not equipment. Exact starting amounts are story-dependent and not confirmed pre-launch.

Can you make money without heists in GTA 6?

Yes — vehicle theft, street racing, ATM hacking, hunting, fishing (catch-and-release for reputation), and treasure diving all provide income outside of heist missions.

Is it better to save cash or deposit in GTA 6?

Always deposit cash at an ATM or through Buckme after every score. Carrying large amounts of cash risks losing it all on death or arrest — the three-currency system punishes players who don\u0027t move money up the chain.
