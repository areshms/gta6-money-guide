How to Make Money in GTA 6 Story Mode

Last updated: November 16, 2026

GTA 6 story mode offers a wider range of money-making activities than any previous GTA. Jason and Lucia bring different strengths, and the game economy rewards players who diversify their income sources.

Character Strengths

Jason
  • Street racing access (Chapter 2+)
  • Vehicle theft rings from car-enthusiast background
  • Hunting contacts (Mount Kalaga)
  • Higher-tier heist crew connections
Lucia
  • Retail theft pipeline from prior connections
  • Small-scale fraud jobs
  • Fence network access
  • Front business management skills

Money Doesn't Expire

Unlike GTA Online where Rockstar inflated prices to maintain grind value, GTA 6 story mode gives purpose to wealth accumulation. The $10M final heist, property purchases, and the money laundering system all create meaningful spending loops. Money earned in Chapter 2 through street racing can fund the auto shop in Chapter 3 that accelerates wealth through Chapter 5.
