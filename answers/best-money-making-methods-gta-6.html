Method Details

Heists

GTA 6 features six major heists plus procedurally generated minor scores. Each has a planning board at your safehouse where you choose approach (stealth vs loud), hire crew, and purchase equipment. Crew members level up with repeated hiring, taking smaller cuts and performing better.

Vehicle Theft (Auto Shop)

Buy the auto shop for $120,000 after the Port Vice heist. The car-scanner app shows which vehicles buyers want. Condition affects payout — deliver undamaged for full value. Range: $15,000–$40,000 per job.

Passive Income Businesses

Nightclubs auto-generate $3,500/hour. Laundromats ($85,000) process $10,000/day in dirty cash. The Nail Salon chain (6 locations, $300,000 total) launders $50,000/day while turning a profit. Businesses accumulate income for up to 3 real-world hours offline.

Priority Spending Order

  1. Masks and spare outfits (reduces police ID risk)
  2. A reliable personal vehicle (your mobile armoury and getaway)
  3. Auto shop ($120,000) — unlocks steady semi-passive income
  4. Laundromat ($85,000) — enables safe cash conversion
  5. Nightclub ($250,000) — unlocks DJ missions + passive income
Pro tip: Stack passive income businesses early. They accumulate while you actively grind heists, effectively doubling your effective income per hour.

Methods to Avoid for Efficiency

  • Destroying stolen cars during getaways (reduced payout)
  • Getting busted with a full cash wallet
  • Over-escalating robberies (killing raises heat unnecessarily)
  • Repeating the same store (diminishing returns from police attention)
