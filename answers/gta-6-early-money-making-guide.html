GTA 6 Early Money Making Guide — First 5 Hours

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The first 5 hours of GTA 6 set the financial trajectory for your entire playthrough. This guide covers every early money method, the optimal purchase order, and traps to avoid as a beginner.

First 60 Minutes: The $10K Start

Minute 0-15: Complete prologue. Do not spend any starting cash on cosmetics.
Minute 15-40: Run the Vice Beach convenience store circuit (8 stores). Wear a mask, crack safes, deposit immediately.
Minute 40-60: Buy a suppressed pistol and a gym membership. Remaining cash goes to a personal vehicle fund.

Hours 1-5: Building Your Base

Hour 1-2: Unlock Chapter 2. Start street racing (Jason). The Vice Beach Loop C-class race ($500 buy-in, $2,500 payout, ~3 min) is your best repeatable earner.
Hour 2-3: Combine street racing with retail theft for Lucia. Use the theft-to-fence pipeline for passive parallel income.
Hour 3-5: Target $30,000–$40,000 for a B-class qualifying vehicle and basic heist prep supplies for Chapter 3.

Early Purchase Priority

  1. Mask and spare outfits ($200–$500) — prevents police ID
  2. Suppressed pistol ($2,000–$3,000) — enables safe camera disabling
  3. Gym membership ($500) — permanent health and stamina gains
  4. Personal vehicle ($5,000–$15,000) — mobile armoury and getaway
  5. B-class car for racing ($15,000–$25,000) — unlocks higher-paying races

New Player Traps to Avoid

  • Buying premier vehicles or properties — they don't earn you anything
  • Carrying large cash stacks — die once and lose hours of progress
  • Over-escalating store robberies — killing the clerk raises heat without extra payout
  • Skipping the auto shop after Port Vice — it's the fastest early passive income unlock

Golden rule for the opening hours: Every dollar spent should either protect money already earned or unlock a new earning method. If a purchase does neither, it can wait.
