How to Get Rich in GTA 6 — The Complete Path to Millions

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Becoming a millionaire in GTA 6 requires more than grinding one method. You need a progression strategy that combines active earning, business investment, and smart financial management. This guide maps the complete journey.

The Wealth Tiers

Tier 1 — Small Time ($0–$50,000) · Chapters 1–2
Focus: Street robberies, ATM hacking, street racing. Avoid cosmetic purchases. Every dollar goes to income-generating assets.
Tier 2 — Operator ($50,000–$500,000) · Chapter 3
Complete the Port Vice heist. Buy auto shop ($120,000). Start vehicle theft missions. This is where compound growth begins.
Tier 3 — Business Owner ($500,000–$2M) · Chapters 4–5
Buy nightclub ($250,000) and laundromat ($85,000). Run Cartel Connection missions. Invest in LCN stocks aligned with story missions.
Tier 4 — Crime Lord ($2M–$10M+) · Chapter 6+
Full passive portfolio (5+ businesses). Maxed heist crew. Stock market timing. Final heist caps the story at $10,000,000.

Critical Wealth Rules

  • Deposit constantly — Cash on person is lost on death. Hit ATMs after every score.
  • Buy income assets first — Vehicles, weapons, and cosmetics only after your businesses are running.
  • Level your crew — Re-hiring the same heist crew reduces their cut and improves performance.
  • Launder before large deposits — Don't trigger the AML flags without a laundromat to clean your cash.

When to Stop Grinding

Once you have 5+ businesses generating $15,000+/hour passive income and a $2M+ stock portfolio, active grinding becomes optional. At this point you can focus on story completion, exploration, and the $10M final heist.

⚠ Common pitfall: Spending on luxury vehicles and properties before establishing passive income. A $2M penthouse doesn't earn you anything — that money in a nightclub and laundromat generates $5,000+/hour.
