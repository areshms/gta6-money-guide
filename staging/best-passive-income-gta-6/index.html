Maximizing Offline Income

Businesses accumulate income for up to 3 real-world hours when you're not playing. To maximize: log in twice daily to collect and trigger the next accumulation period. With 5+ businesses, this generates $45,000–$90,000 per day in effortless income. Over a week of checking in twice daily: $315,000–$630,000 without a minute of active play.
