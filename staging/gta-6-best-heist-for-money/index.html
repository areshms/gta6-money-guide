Heist Preparation Tips

  • Always scope the location first — higher-tier robbery targets show estimated payout on the map
  • Level your crew: the same gunman improves every time you hire them
  • Choose approach based on equipment — stealth costs less prep time but requires silenced weapons
  • Stockpile armor and ammo before starting prep missions
  • Don't skip the escape vehicle — a fast getaway car prevents losing loot
