The Optimal Purchase Order

  1. Auto shop ($120,000) — Buy immediately after the Port Vice heist. Fastest ROI in the game.
  2. Laundromat ($85,000) — Essential before you start making large deposits from heists.
  3. Nightclub ($250,000) — Unlocks passive income + DJ recruitment missions worth $125,000 total.
  4. Nail Salons (chain, $300,000) — Max laundering capacity for endgame wealth.
  5. Arcade / Cash Lab — Only after the above four. These expand income but require existing infrastructure.

The Laundering Pipeline

Without laundromats, depositing $50,000+ in a single transaction triggers the game's AML system. At flag 3, FBI audits freeze all business income and seize a percentage of banked funds. The laundering hierarchy is:

  • 1 laundromat: safe up to ~$70K/week in dirty cash
  • 1 nightclub: safe up to ~$175K/week
  • 6 nail salons + nightclub: safe up to ~$600K/week

Buy your laundering capacity before you need it — the game doesn't warn you before flag 2 triggers the freeze.
